it is your primary job as a founder to find people who care about the problem you're solving, tell them about your solution, and convert them to get sales.

one reliable method that has been working well is to steal engagement from people talking about the exact problem I'm solving already.

i.e. using social media to find people who've engaged with posts on my topic and reaching out to them. Here's how that worked:

<figure><img src="{{ '/assets/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude-2.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="GTM hack: steal engagement with Claude" style="max-width: 30em; width: 100%;"></figure>

almost a 2.5x better response rate!

Tbh, this almost feels like a cheat code: these people are already interested in the topic and ready to talk.

<!--more-->

The first step is to find the correct people, here's two ways to do that

**1. Steal engagement from competitors**

One easy way to do that is to go to your competitor's posts and find their best posts.
(Fundraising posts are great for this. They get 1000s of people liking and commenting.)
So your GTM move is easy: Steal all of that traffic from them :)

<figure><img src="{{ '/assets/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude-3.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="GTM hack: steal engagement with Claude" style="max-width: 30em; width: 100%;"></figure>

Go check out your competitor's linkedin pages, I'm sure you'll find something relevant

**1b. Or, source engagement from your best posts**

...but competitors aren't your only source.
Have you had any good posts get a lot of likes recently?
If yes, did you message all of the people who engaged there?
If not, you are basically losing out on free money.
Dig through your past posts for people who know about you already and send them all connection requests + messages.

<figure><img src="{{ '/assets/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude-4.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="GTM hack: steal engagement with Claude" style="max-width: 30em; width: 100%;"></figure>

Since these folks know you already, they trust you a bit more, and will be willing to talk to you.

**2. Send them a value-adding message**

now, an easy mistake you can make is just to ask them to for the sale directly. Don't do this, that's too aggressive.

<figure><img src="{{ '/assets/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude-5.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="GTM hack: steal engagement with Claude" style="max-width: 30em; width: 100%;"></figure>

Instead, try to continue the conversation by making it about the topic and idea as a whole.
One trick is to send them a relevant blog post or article on the topic and kick a discussion off that way.

<figure><img src="{{ '/assets/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude/gtm-hack-steal-engagement-with-claude-6.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="GTM hack: steal engagement with Claude" style="max-width: 30em; width: 100%;"></figure>

Whatever you choose, try making it sound natural rather than too salesy or pushy. People don't like being sold to or pitched - but they do like discussing topics that are interesting to them.
